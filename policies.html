SherenVogue Support & Policies

1. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Global Shipping, Uncompromised Luxury — At SherenVogue, we ensure that your premium leather goods, watches, and accessories reach you securely and promptly, no matter where you are in the world.

Order Processing:

All orders are carefully inspected, beautifully packaged, and dispatched within 1-2 business days of order confirmation.

Domestic Shipping (Bangladesh):

  • Delivery Time: 2-4 business days.
  • Payment Options: Cash on Delivery (COD) and all major secure online payment methods are available.

International Shipping:

  • Delivery Time: 5-7 business days via our premium global delivery partners (e.g., DHL, FedEx).
  • Tracking: A live tracking link will be provided via email the moment your order is dispatched.

Customs, Duties & Taxes:

For international orders, please note that local customs duties or taxes mandated by your country's regulations are not included in the checkout price and remain the responsibility of the customer.

2. Return Policy

Our Commitment to Your Satisfaction — We stand behind the craftsmanship of every SherenVogue product. If your purchase does not perfectly meet your expectations, we offer a seamless return process.

Return Window:

You may initiate a return within 14 days of receiving your delivery.

Condition of Returned Items:

To be eligible for a return, the item must be unworn, unused, and in the exact pristine condition you received it. All original luxury packaging, warranty cards, protective films (for watches), and brand tags must be fully intact.

Non-Returnable Items:

For hygiene and quality assurance purposes, opened or sprayed perfumes, and custom-engraved or personalized accessories cannot be returned.

How to Initiate a Return:

Simply email our concierge team at support@sherenvogue.com with your Order Number and the reason for the return. We will guide you through the next steps and provide the necessary return shipping details.

3. Refund Policy

Transparent & Secure Refunds

Inspection Process:

Once your returned item is received at our facility, our quality control team will inspect it within 2 business days. We will immediately send you an email notifying you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

Refund Issuance:

If approved, your refund will be processed automatically to your original method of payment. Please allow 5-7 business days for the funds to appear in your account, depending on your bank or credit card issuer.

Cash on Delivery (COD) Refunds:

For domestic COD orders, refunds will be issued via secure Bank Transfer or Mobile Financial Services (MFS) as per your preference.

Shipping Costs:

Original shipping charges are non-refundable. If you are shipping an item back from outside Bangladesh, you will be responsible for paying the return shipping costs, which will be deducted from your total refund amount.
